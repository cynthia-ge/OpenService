Account access

Making an account inactive blocks sign-in and API access and signs out its browsers on their next request. Records and history remain available. Reactivation requires a fresh sign-in.

Changing a role signs out this person’s browsers on their next request. Basic API access uses the new role immediately. All roles can read workspace-visible records. Private records require explicit access.

Assisted password recovery

Verify the person’s identity through your agreed support process before sharing a link. Links last 15 minutes and work once. Issuing a new link replaces the previous one; it does not change the current password or sign the person out.

This recovery link is shown once. Share it privately with the verified account holder.

Group access

Review this person’s groups before offboarding. Removing all memberships withdraws group-restricted record access on the next request. Account status, role and private-record ownership are managed separately. Reactivation does not restore removed memberships.

Private record handover

live private records belong to this account.

Transfer access to all of these records and their attachments to a different active account. They remain private; the previous owner loses access on the next request. Administrators retain access. Original creators, business assignments and history are preserved. Already-deleted records, group access and workspace records are excluded. Deactivate departing accounts separately.
